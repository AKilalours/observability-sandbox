O(v;G) · nuScenes 6-camera rig · 0.4 m grid

An ego-frame bird's-eye view, the way a perception stack sees one. The ego stays at the origin and the world moves past it. Traffic follows lanes, keeps headway under a car-following model and obeys the signal. Every cell is rescored live by the measure from the paper: angular coverage from each camera multiplied by first-hit visibility along a marched ray. Where the cameras can resolve the surface you see road. Where they cannot it goes dark, whatever the network believes is there.
